How the maths works

We build your rides on the selected dates and UK local times, then compare full purchases across that period. A 7-day estimate pays the full monthly membership fee if you choose a membership. Monthly renewals follow the purchase date, rather than a fractional weeks-per-month approximation.

Minute bundles activate just before a planned ride. The calculator chooses when to buy that same bundle again or pay standard fares, respecting both minutes and rolling expiry. If a bundle runs out mid-ride, remaining time uses the standard minute rate unless you enable same-bundle auto-reload. Once a bundle is exhausted, later rides pay the normal unlock fee. Early manual top-ups, overlapping different bundles and group rides are not modelled.

Unused minutes are separated into minutes that expire within your period and minutes still valid afterwards. Those remaining minutes are not credited against the purchase price. Estimates assume no existing pass balance, promotions, refunds, parking penalties, deposits or extra fees.

Short-ride offers use actual duration including your parking allowance; minute billing rounds each ride up when enabled. The what-if range changes all rides by your chosen amount. It is a sensitivity test, not a probability forecast. Annual equivalents simply scale this period to 365.25 days.

Pricing sources & what we checked

Charging rules reviewed 30 October 2026. Lime does not publish a single guaranteed UK tariff. City, vehicle, time and account offers can differ. Edit the rates and turn off any pass you cannot buy.

  • Lime’s pass and membership terms: expiry, rounding, renewal, optional auto-reload and restrictions on combining products.
  • Lime’s billing guide: local rates and whole-minute billing.
  • Lime’s 2026 LimePrime update: flat-rate rides up to 20 minutes and a cheaper short-ride tier.
  • DASH’s own LimePrime offering, 4 March 2026: London £6.99 monthly, £1 up to 5 minutes, £1.70 up to 20 minutes.
  • Independent London in-app price observations, 26 October 2026: sample £1 + 31p/min, £4.59 / £7.69 / £19.94 / £36.99 bundles and 16p/min LimePrime overage. These are reference inputs, not a live operator price feed. A reported PAYG short-ride offer is available as an opt-in setting.
  • Dott’s minute-pass announcement: 30-minute / 24-hour and 100-minute / 7-day structure. Dott price amounts and membership offers are retained from the original adviser as unverified examples.
  • Travelwest’s 6 May 2026 announcement: WEST SummerPass and Sunday unlock promotion. Both are off by default; enable only if currently offered. WEST SummerPass does not auto-renew.

Eligible riders may have access to Lime Access or local concessions. Enter those offers separately using the custom-plan fields. Do not assume discounts can be stacked.
